astrology and offering intuitive guidance through the natural cycles, have been part of my work since early. I taught psychological astrology from 2014 to 2020 on in-person long term courses in the Huber School in Barcelona, which was a beautiful container. since I started my practice, I have worked offering 1:1 readings and guidance extensively, for more than 15 years.

I have facilitated retreats for women on sexuality and sovereign feminine wisdom, as well as astrology and nature cycles retreats. all of it has given me a lot of experiential knowledge and embodied wisdom in this work, and the opportunity to develop my own interconnected approach and framework.

being a mother and diving deep into motherhood has been my foundation. alchemising trauma and transforming highly dysfunctional patterns through self-responsibility, consciousness and somatic practices have lead me to more freedom, more eros and creation in my life.

I am passionate about sovereign birth and rewilding motherhood, rooted in the remembrance of the true intuition and innate biological wisdom that we as women carry in ourselves, in attunement with the earth. I support women through the motherhood continuum, pregnancy, birth and postpartum.
